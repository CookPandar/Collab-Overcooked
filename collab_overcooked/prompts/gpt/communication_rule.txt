Input
-----
For every timestep you receive a full snapshot containing:
1. The last three decision records, each with:
   - `Order`: the current recipe to complete.
   - `Scene`: status of every ingredient and utensil.
   - `Agent State`: what you and your teammate are holding or doing.
   - `Past conversation turns`: chronological dialogue such as  
     `{teammate} says (turn 1): ...` / `{role} says (turn 1): ...` etc.  
     Read from top to bottom; the newest message appears last. You must answer your teammate's latest turn.
2. `Recent Goal`: the multi-step plan you produced previously.

Output
------
Produce exactly three fields in order:
```
Think: ...
Recent Goal: ...
Action: ...
```


### Think
- Reason step by step about the environment, recipe progress, and teammate messages.
- Respect the utensil constraints of `{role}` and `{teammate}` spaces.
- If a step must be executed by your teammate, wrap it inside `request(...)` while reasoning.

### Recent Goal
- Keep the previous goal if it is still valid.
- If it is finished or no longer appropriate, overwrite it with an updated multi-step plan.


### Action
- Each timestep you may output only one action, either a collaboration primitive (`Collab(...)`) or a physical action, but never both. Collaboration requests and replies do **not** consume timesteps-once you issue a `Collab`, you can treat it as immediately receiving the teammate's response.
  1. **If the current focus is coordinating with your teammate**, output `Collab(...)` as the sole action for this timestep.  
     - Allowed cooperative primitives: `request`, `seek`, `deny`, `ack`. Within a single `Collab`, choose exactly one type of primitive; only `request` may appear multiple times separated by semicolons.  
     - Example: `Collab(request(pickup(bell_pepper, ingredient_dispenser));request(place_obj_on_counter()))` or `Collab(ack(Chef))`.  
     - After the collaboration request, wait for the teammate's reply; during the next timestep decide whether to continue collaborating or switch to executing your own action.
  2. **If no additional collaboration is required at this moment**, output a physical action that belongs to your skill set (e.g., `pickup(...)`, `put_obj_in_utensil(...)`).  
     - Once collaboration is finished and the teammate has responded, the following timesteps must reassess whether to collaborate again or proceed with physical actions, ensuring only one action per timestep.
